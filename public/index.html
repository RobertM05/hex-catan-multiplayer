Game Rules

Resources: Wood (Forest), Brick (Hills), Wool (Pasture), Wheat (Fields), Ore (Mountains). The desert produces no resources.
Building costs: Road = 1 Wood + 1 Brick. Settlement = 1 Wood + 1 Brick + 1 Wool + 1 Wheat (1 VP). City = 3 Ore + 2 Wheat (2 VP, replaces an existing settlement). Development Card = 1 Ore + 1 Wool + 1 Wheat.
Distance rule: Any two settlements or cities must be at least 2 intersections apart.
The 7 & the Robber: If the dice sum is 7, players with more than 7 cards in hand discard half. The active player moves the robber onto a hex and may take 1 resource from a player with an adjacent settlement or city.
Special titles: Longest Road (at least 5 continuous segments) = +2 VP. Largest Army (at least 3 knights played) = +2 VP.
Victory: First to 13 VP wins, but no one may win until after the first barbarian attack. Classic development cards and Largest Army are not used.
Commodities: Cities on wool/ore/wood produce 1 resource + 1 commodity (cloth/coin/paper). Cities on wheat/brick produce 2 resources. Settlements never produce commodities. Commodities count toward the 7-card discard limit and can be traded, but cannot pay for buildings.
Building costs: Road = 1 Wood + 1 Brick. Settlement = 1 Wood + 1 Brick + 1 Wool + 1 Wheat (1 VP). City = 3 Ore + 2 Wheat (2 VP). Knight = 1 Ore + 1 Wool (placed inactive). Activate knight = 1 Wheat. Promote knight = 1 Wool + 1 Ore. City wall = 2 Brick.
Distance rule: Any two settlements or cities must be at least 2 intersections apart.
The 7 & the Robber: Until barbarians have attacked once, a 7 only forces discards — no robber move or steal. After the first attack, a 7 works as in the base game: discard, then move the robber and steal.
Knights: Supply per player: 2 Basic, 2 Strong, 1 Mighty. Basic knights promote to Strong with no Politics requirement. Fortress (Politics 3) unlocks Mighty. Active knights defend against barbarians, can move, displace a weaker enemy knight, and chase the robber after the first barbarian attack (then become inactive).
Event die & barbarians: Each roll uses 2 production dice plus an event die (3/6 barbarian, 1/6 each Trade/Politics/Science). The barbarian ship advances on a barbarian face. At the end of the track, if active knight strength is at least the number of cities, Catan wins and the unique strongest defender gets Defender of Catan (+1 VP). If Catan loses, players with the fewest active knights who still have a city must downgrade one. All knights then become inactive. A color face lets every player whose matching track is at least the red die draw a progress card.
City improvements: Need a city to invest. Cost equals the level you reach (1–5) in cloth (Trade), coin (Politics), or paper (Science). Draw a progress card at level 3. Unique leader at level 4+ places a metropolis (+2 VP). Aqueduct (Science 3) lets you choose 1 resource when a production roll yields nothing. Level 5 unlocks special perks: 2:1 bank trade (Trade) or Mighty Knights (Politics). Walls raise the discard threshold by 2 each (3 walls max).
Special titles: Longest Road (at least 5 continuous segments) = +2 VP. Defender of Catan cards = +1 VP each. There is no Largest Army.
Progress cards: Replace development cards. Hand limit 4 unplayed cards. Play on your turn (Alchemist before you roll).
